About Manika

Ten years of GTM work, now built into software.

I work at the intersection of go-to-market strategy and AI implementation. Most consultants hand you a plan and leave the execution to your team. I do both: I diagnose where your marketing and sales workflows are burning time, design the plan to fix it, and then build the actual AI-enabled tools that carry it out — lead qualification, outreach sequencing, reporting, campaign operations, and the rest of the repeatable work that shouldn't need a human doing it manually every day.

The result is a GTM function where your team spends its time on strategy, relationships and judgment calls — the parts that genuinely need a person — while the systems handle everything that repeats.
